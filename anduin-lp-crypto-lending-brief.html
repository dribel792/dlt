Confidential · Liquidity Provider Brief

Secured crypto lending to Revolut retail clients
Structure, fund flows & risk profile

DLT-Anduin (legal entity TBD) is the lender of record for crypto-denominated margin loans offered to Revolut retail clients in the UK and EEA. This brief explains where LP capital sits at every point, how loans are secured and liquidated, and the loss-absorbing layers that sit in front of LP principal.

Prepared by DLT-Anduin Platform partner Revolut Ltd (UK) / Revolut Digital Assets Europe Ltd (EEA) Assets USDC · BTC · ETH · SOL Version v0.1 · Nov 2026 · draft, subject to final contract terms

1Summary

The LP funds a closed-loop programme: capital only ever moves between the LP and DLT-Anduin's dedicated account at Revolut. Every loan is fully secured by the asset the client buys, plus the client's own deposit as first loss. Loans are liquidated automatically and backed by Revolut-funded protection layers before the LP is exposed.

Closed loop
One whitelisted route in, one out. LP ⇄ DLT-Anduin account at Revolut, nothing else.
In-kind
Loans are repaid in the asset that was lent. No price exposure on principal.
5% / 17.5%
Revolut covers losses up to a 5% price move past the liquidation trigger. If it liquidates later than 120 s, that cover rises to 17.5% (BTC; 20% ETH, 22.5% SOL). The LP only loses beyond that, and only once the Liquidation Fund is used up.
120 s
Contractual liquidation SLA. Revolut pays for any delay beyond it.

Why the risk is low

1 · Collateral first

The borrowed funds and the client's own deposit stay in a Revolut margin account, held for the benefit of DLT-Anduin. The client can't withdraw them while a loan is outstanding, and they can't be used for anything else. cl. 8.1–8.2

2 · Automated liquidation

Revolut checks every portfolio every second. A liquidation fires after 3 consecutive ticks below maintenance margin and must be executed within 120 seconds. Sch. 3

3 · Revolut guarantee

If the liquidation price falls below the client's bankruptcy price, Revolut pays the shortfall up to a fixed cap. If Revolut breaches the 120 s SLA, it pays execution losses up to a much higher cap. Revolut's own balance sheet backs these payments. Sch. 3

4 · Liquidation Fund

A Revolut-owned fund, filled with client liquidation fees, sits in the repayment waterfall for any remaining shortfall. Its target size grows with the loan book. cl. 9

3Where LP capital sits: fund flow

LP capital is always in one of four places: (a) undrawn at the LP, (b) in DLT-Anduin's R4B account, (c) in DLT-Anduin's Reserve or Lending Pool at Revolut, or (d) lent to a client and secured by collateral in a Revolut margin account. (b), (c) and (d) are all inside Revolut's custody perimeter.

Figure 1: Capital locations and permitted movements. No arrow leaves the closed loop: Revolut moves assets only between DLT-Anduin's own accounts and secured client loans.

  1. Draw. Revolut sends an automated Pull Request when DLT-Anduin's R4B balance can't cover a pool top-up. DLT-Anduin passes it to the LP, and the LP delivers to the single whitelisted address, DLT-Anduin's R4B deposit address. Revolut accepts deliveries only from wallet addresses agreed in writing. cl. 6.1, 6.5, 7.2(f), Sch. 2
  2. Allocate. Revolut moves assets from the R4B account into the Reserve Pool up to the Schedule 1 level (currently GBP 7.5m equivalent). cl. 7.2(c), Sch. 1
  3. Top up. When a Lending Pool falls below its Lower Threshold, Revolut refills it from the R4B account or the Reserve Pool. The threshold is dynamic: when utilisation is <65% for a day or >90% for an hour, it resets to max(17% of outstanding loans, 200k USDC). cl. 7.2(a),(d),(g)
  4. Lend. When a client opens a margin trade, the loan goes from the Lending Pool into the client's Margin Account. The loan proceeds (e.g. the BTC bought) stay there as collateral alongside the client's own deposit. cl. 8.1, def. "Collateral"
  5. Repay. When the position closes, or is liquidated, principal goes back to the Lending Pool in the asset that was lent. DLT-Anduin's share of client interest is credited to the Lending Pool too. cl. 11.1–11.2
  6. Return. When a Lending Pool exceeds its Upper Threshold, Revolut sends the surplus back to DLT-Anduin's R4B account. From there, assets go back only to the LP's whitelisted address. cl. 7.2(b)
Closed-loop controls offered to the LP. (i) The LP-side facility account has exactly one whitelisted withdrawal address: DLT-Anduin's R4B deposit address. (ii) DLT-Anduin's R4B account is contractually limited to this programme. (iii) DLT-Anduin whitelists only the LP deposit address for outbound transfers from R4B. (iv) DLT-Anduin can't withdraw manually from the pools; assets leave them only through Revolut's automated rebalancing. cl. 6.2–6.3

5Life of a client loan

Worked example: a client deposits 100 USDC and borrows 900 USDC to buy 1,000 USDC of BTC (10× notional). Maintenance margin ratio (MMR) is 3%.

  1. Onboarding. Revolut onboards the client and runs KYC/CDD. The client accepts DLT-Anduin's Client Terms, so DLT-Anduin becomes the lender of record. No credit check is needed: the loan is secured purely by collateral. cl. 4.2, 5.3
  2. Draw. 900 USDC moves from DLT-Anduin's USDC Lending Pool into the client's Margin Account. The client buys 1,000 USDC of BTC (900 borrowed + 100 own).
  3. Lock. The full 1,000 USDC of BTC is collateral held for DLT-Anduin's benefit. The client can't withdraw it, and it can't be used for anything else. cl. 8.2
  4. Monitor. Every second Revolut computes Margin Balance = assets × (1 − haircut) − (loan + accrued interest) and compares it with Maintenance Margin = position value × MMR.
  5. Close. When the client closes, the position is sold. The 900 USDC principal and accrued interest go back first; the client gets the remainder. Revolut collects interest and credits DLT-Anduin's share to the Lending Pool. cl. 11
  6. Liquidate. If the margin balance stays below the maintenance margin for 3 consecutive seconds, liquidation is triggered (t₁). With 900 debt and a 3% MMR, that happens at a position value of ≈ 927.8 (equity 27.8 = 3% of 927.8). Revolut must execute within 120 seconds (t₂). Sch. 3

Why the liquidation timer matters. The SLA separates market risk, which is a fast gap the engine can't outrun, from operational risk, which covers outages, slow execution and thin order books. Revolut fully owns operational risk up to LC₂. That converts the most common real-world cause of lender losses on margin books into a Revolut obligation.

7The Liquidation Fund (insurance fund) explained

What it is. A pool that pays when a liquidated account's collateral doesn't fully cover the loan. It works like the insurance fund on a derivatives exchange: profitable liquidations pay into it, and bad debt from gapped liquidations is paid out of it. Losses from individual accounts are mutualised across the whole book instead of hitting the lender.

How it fills. Clients pay a liquidation fee when they are liquidated. While the fund is below its Minimum Fund Threshold, 100% of those fees go into it. cl. 9.6

How big it gets. The target scales with the book: Minimum Fund Threshold = 10% × 90th-percentile aggregate open-position value over the last 12 months. Example: if open positions peak around GBP 50m, the target is about GBP 5m.

Above the target. Revolut may withdraw anything above the threshold as its own profit. So the threshold works as a target level: the fund keeps rebuilding towards it but isn't built up indefinitely.

Ownership. Revolut owns and manages the fund, as a single consolidated fund for UK and EEA. It sits in front of the lender in the repayment order: collateral → Liquidation Fund → SLA compensation. cl. 9.1, 9.3

Start-up. The fund starts at zero on the Effective Date and grows with activity. In the early phase, lender protection comes mainly from collateral and Revolut's SLA guarantee, which Revolut's own funds back from day one.

Overall: low risk. The LP's principal is senior to client collateral, a Revolut-funded market-loss tranche, a mutualised Liquidation Fund and a Revolut-funded operational-loss tranche. A loss needs a >5% move in BTC (or the relevant asset) inside the 2-minute execution window and an exhausted Liquidation Fund at the same time. Historically, moves of that speed have been limited to a handful of market-wide dislocations.

9Economics

Paid on drawn loans

DLT-Anduin receives a share of client interest on every loan it funds. Retail margin rates sit well above wholesale crypto lending rates.

Volume floor
