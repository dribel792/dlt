How to read this

  • Workstreams 1–5 are independent product lines. This view stacks them end-to-end — one squad, one after another, Sep 2026 → early 2028.
  • Dashed bars are tasks that overlap inside a workstream (e.g. ETP backoffice, Structured-Products backoffice).
  • 2.3 → 2.4 and 3.2 → 3.3 run consecutively, not in parallel — reflected in the widened ETP and RWA bands.
  • Perps is the long pole at ~6 months, driven almost entirely by the 4-month Risk Engine integration.

Assumptions & open items

  • ETP 2.1 (Backoffice Adaption) had no duration given — placeholdered (~0.7 mo) and flagged. Needs sizing.
  • 4 months allotted for Risk Engine Integration (1.3) — the largest single task.
  • The ~6-month parallel figure in our ask assumes ~5 independent squads; shared platform/backoffice teams could create soft dependencies not shown here.
  • Structured Products (4) assumes 4.3 Risk Engine follows the per-product build; 4.2 runs parallel to 4.1.1.
