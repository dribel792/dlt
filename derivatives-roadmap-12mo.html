Use Case 01

Bit2Me — breadth & liquidity

DLT Finance

What they want

  • Crypto derivatives at launch — the immediate, proven demand.
  • Rapid expansion into equity derivatives — they don't see crypto and equities as separate businesses.
  • Liquidity is sourced from several different venues — they need us to aggregate and route, not just connect a single book.
  • Later stage: structured option strategies — covered calls (yield) and shark fin (capped upside, protected downside).

What it means for us

Read-through

Bit2Me is the clearest case for a liquidity aggregation + smart order routing layer across heterogeneous sources, and for treating equity delta-1 as a first-class product alongside crypto.

The structured-product ask is real but later — it should not pull engineering focus from the equity-rails work that unblocks revenue first.

Liquidity aggregation / SOR Equity delta-1 trading Structured products (later) Pricing engine (later)

Use Case 02

Vancelian — the risk-engine forcing function

DLT Finance

What they want

  • Primarily equity perps with EUR/USD as both collateral and settlement currency.
  • Also crypto perps — same collateral and settlement currency (one wallet, two asset classes).
  • Intro came via d2x, a MiFID-regulated MTF, who expect flow routed to them.
  • Strong interest in yield and downside-protection option strategies.

The blocker

Why this is decisive

d2x does not offer subaccount structures. That means we cannot lean on the venue to segregate and risk-manage client positions — we have to bring our own risk engine.

The same engine that solves d2x also delivers the thing all three clients want: one cross-margined account spanning equity and crypto perps, in a single collateral currency.

Risk engine (own) Cross-margin equity + crypto Multi-currency collateral (EUR/USD) Option strategies

Conclusion

What we build — and what we buy

DLT Finance
1

Risk Engine — the keystone

Our own cross-margin risk engine, spanning equity + crypto perps in one collateral currency. Build, with a licensed margin/VaR library to accelerate.

Appears in every client's blocker list · turns the d2x subaccount gap into owned infrastructure
2

Equity Delta-1 + Nexus

Tokenized-equity trading engine plus Nexus for post-trade, settlement and MiFID/EMIR reporting. Build.

Unlocks the equity expansion all three clients are heading toward

OMS & fee engine + liquidity routing

Multi-tenant order management, flexible fee schedules, and aggregation across liquidity sources. Build core, integrate connectors.

Pricing engine + option-strategy lifecycle (covered call, shark fin, PPN), reported through Nexus. Buy/integrate the pricer, build the wrapper.

High-margin upsell · everyone wants it, but it's Phase 3 — don't build the pricer from scratch

Open Question

What else would sharpen the offer?

DLT Finance

Beyond the must-builds — candidates that would meaningfully widen the moat. Flagging for the team to prioritise.

1
Funding-rate engine for equity perps

Funding that reflects equity carry — borrow cost & dividends — not just a crypto-style basis.

2
Corporate-actions service

Dividends, splits, mergers on tokenized equity. Easy to forget, breaks delta-1 if missing.

3
Liquidation engine + insurance fund

Auto-deleveraging and a backstop fund — required once we own the risk.

4
Cross-currency margining

EUR collateral against USD-denominated risk, with FX haircuts.

5
White-label / multi-tenant config

Per-client risk, fee and product params — these are all B2B2X partners.

6
Real-time risk dashboard + kill switch

Live exposure view and a hard stop the partner desk can trust.

7
Institutional connectivity (FIX / API)

Low-latency access for routed flow — especially the d2x relationship.

8
Best-execution & transaction reporting

MiFIR/EMIR obligations baked into Nexus from day one, not bolted on.
