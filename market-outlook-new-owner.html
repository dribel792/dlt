Where the crypto market is right now

A softer directional market, but two structural openings — one regulatory, one institutional.

The crypto market in mid-2026 is in a regime shift. Spot volumes across European retail venues are materially below their 2024 peak. Funding rates on major perps have compressed. Memes and high-beta narratives are recycling, but without the capital inflows of the prior cycle. For brokers and exchanges with a pure directional-crypto product shelf, this is a difficult year.

The underlying structure of the market, however, has improved in two ways that are directly relevant to DLT:

02

B2B2X — the MiCAR wave

A new generation of licensed institutions, all looking for the next product line.

MiCAR has now produced a meaningful cohort of approved CASP licences across the EU. These institutions have spent 18–24 months and significant capital getting authorised, and they have arrived at the same realisation simultaneously: a plain crypto-spot offering is not sufficient to retain or grow a client book in 2026.

What they want is the next product to put in front of their clients. Their licence covers MiCAR; the natural adjacent product set lives under MiFID. Building those products in-house is a multi-year, multi-million regulated build. Plugging into a licensed MiFID counterparty via API is a quarter.

That is the gap DLT sits in.

Why not margin trading?

Margin trading on spot crypto sits in a regulatory grey area under MiCAR — there is no clean, harmonised framework for retail leveraged spot in the EU. Most MiCAR institutions will not touch it without explicit national-regulator guidance. Derivatives, by contrast, sit cleanly under MiFID II — well-understood, passportable, defensible. That is why all eyes are on derivatives.

04

Equity perpetuals — the natural extension

Perp mechanics applied to single names and indices: what crypto users want when crypto is flat.

The retail user who has spent two years trading BTC and ETH perps already understands funding, mark price, liquidation, and leverage. The mechanics translate one-for-one to single-name equity perpetuals: NVDA-PERP, TSLA-PERP, SPX-PERP, GOLD-PERP. Same UX, same risk engine, same liquidation logic — just a different underlying.

This is exactly the product retail wants in a flat-crypto market. They keep the muscle memory and the leverage; the underlying gives them something to trade. For the partner platform, it is incremental product without rebuilding a single front-end component.

Why it's a clean MiFID product

Equity perpetuals can be structured as either (a) derivatives referencing a regulated underlying (cleanest), or (b) securitised perpetuals — factor certificates / mini-futures issued under an EU prospectus and distributed OTC through licensed brokers. DLT covers both paths.

Why partners want it now

Crypto-native venues cannot offer equity exposure without a securities licence. DLT's MiFID stack means a MiCAR platform can offer NVDA, TSLA, SPX exposure to their existing client base on day one — without becoming an investment firm themselves.

05

Yield-focused option strategies

Packaged option strategies that retail can buy in one click — the dominant retail product when direction is sideways.

In a flat market, retail does not stop wanting product — it stops wanting directional product. The dominant retail flow in Asia and in European structured products in 2026 is into yield-enhanced structured strategies: pre-packaged option positions that pay an attractive coupon in exchange for capped upside or conditional downside risk.

DLT's MiFID backbone supports all four of the dominant retail formats. Each is explained below with its payoff profile.

5.1 · Dual Investment / Covered Calls

What it is. The user deposits an asset (e.g. BTC or USDC) and commits to either sell at a higher strike if the underlying rallies, or buy at a lower strike if it dips. In exchange, they earn a fixed, attractive yield (often 15–60% annualised, scaled to the term) for the duration of the product, typically 1–14 days.

How it works under the hood. The product sells an out-of-the-money option (a call if the user deposited the asset; a put if they deposited the quote currency). The premium received is the yield paid to the user. At expiry, if the option is out of the money, the user gets their deposit back plus the yield. If the option is in the money, the user is "settled" into the other asset at the strike — they have effectively sold high or bought low.

Covered Call / Dual Investment payoff — flat yield in the expected range, conversion above the strike.

Why retail loves it. Headline yields look high, the worst case is framed as "you sold your BTC at a higher price than today" or "you bought BTC at a cheaper price than today" — both feel like wins. Binance, OKX, Bybit have made this one of the highest-volume retail products globally.

5.2 · Shark Fin

What it is. A capital-protected structured note that pays an enhanced yield if the underlying stays within a defined range, and a low (or zero) yield if it breaches that range. Capital is returned at maturity in either case — no loss of principal, only of upside.

How it works under the hood. A zero-coupon bond delivers principal protection; the residual premium funds a "knock-out" call spread or put spread. If the underlying stays inside the corridor, the spread pays a high coupon. If it knocks out, the spread expires worthless and only a small "consolation" coupon is paid.

Shark Fin payoff — capital always returned; enhanced yield only if underlying stays inside the corridor.

Why retail loves it. "Capital protected" is the strongest selling angle in any structured product. Pair that with double-digit headline coupons and this is the dominant European retail format outside of pure crypto. Translating it to BTC/ETH/SPX underlyings is what makes DLT's stack uniquely positioned.

5.3 · 0DTE — Zero-Day-To-Expiry options

What it is. Options that expire on the same trading day they are bought. The user picks a strike and a direction, pays a small premium, and either captures a multiple or loses the premium by end of session.

0DTE has been the single fastest-growing product on US options exchanges. SPX 0DTE now exceeds 50% of total SPX option volume on some sessions. The mechanic — pay a small fixed premium, get binary-style exposure to a one-day move — translates directly to crypto and to single-name equity perpetual references.

0DTE call payoff — small fixed cost, leveraged exposure to a single-session move.

Why it matters for DLT. 0DTE is a high-frequency, high-engagement product. It keeps users on platform daily without requiring directional conviction from them. For the partner platform, it dramatically improves session frequency metrics. For DLT, it is incremental order flow into the derivatives stack we already operate.

5.4 · Sprinters / Knock-Out certificates

What it is. A leveraged participation certificate with an automatic knock-out barrier. The user takes a directional view with embedded leverage (5x–50x). If the underlying moves in their direction, the certificate tracks the move on a leveraged basis. If the underlying touches a pre-defined barrier, the certificate knocks out and is settled at (or near) zero.

How it differs from a perp. A perp is a continuously rolling, cash-settled futures contract with funding payments. A sprinter is a securitised instrument — a certificate issued under an EU prospectus with an ISIN, distributed OTC through MiFID-licensed brokers. The leverage and knock-out are baked into the structure. There is no funding payment and no margin call: maximum loss is capped at the premium paid.

Long Sprinter / Knock-Out certificate — leveraged participation until barrier; instant knock-out below barrier.

Why retail loves it. Sprinters are the dominant retail leveraged product in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the Nordics — total volume across European exchanges runs into hundreds of billions of EUR per year. The product is familiar, accepted by regulators, and well-understood. Putting BTC, ETH, SPX, single-name equity sprinters in front of a retail MiCAR client base is a clean product extension.

Why this matters for DLT

These four formats — Dual Investment, Shark Fin, 0DTE, Sprinters — cover the full retail yield + leveraged spectrum. They all run on the same derivatives and options primitives DLT already manages. They all distribute through the same MiCAR-platform / MiFID-broker pipe DLT has already built. They are not four new businesses — they are four configurations of the same business.

B2B — liquidity provision & best execution

The institutional side. Below-capacity tier-1 desks plus ECN distribution keeps this business durable.

The institutional half of the market tells a different story than the retail half. Large bank trading desks have opened crypto lines but are nowhere near full deployment. They cannot yet warehouse meaningful crypto risk, their internal mandates remain conservative, and many do not have direct exchange relationships. That keeps the demand for external liquidity provision and routed best execution structurally elevated.

8.1 · ECN distribution remains the dominant pipe

For institutional crypto flow, three ECNs dominate the market: 360T, oneZero, and Crossover Markets. Every meaningful institutional liquidity programme — including ours — runs through one or more of these. DLT is connected and live on all three.

360T

Deutsche Börse-owned multi-asset venue with the deepest bank connectivity. The institutional default for crypto NDFs and structured flow.

oneZero

Broker-focused infrastructure layer with strong retail-broker connectivity. Where flow from FX-style brokers crosses into the crypto market.

Crossover Markets

Institutional ECN purpose-built for digital assets. Smaller than 360T but with strong relationships in funds & market-makers.

8.2 · Best execution as a moat

MiFID II best-execution obligations are not a checkbox for institutional counterparties — they are an audit requirement. The institutions that can demonstrate routed best execution across multiple venues, with measurable reporting, win the mandates. The institutions that route to a single venue do not. DLT's documented, multi-venue routing is one of the few defensible institutional moats in the European crypto market today.

The point

The B2B side is not a growth story — it is a stability story. ECN flow plus best-execution mandates produce a stable institutional revenue base while the B2B2X retail side is where the upside lives. We hold both.
