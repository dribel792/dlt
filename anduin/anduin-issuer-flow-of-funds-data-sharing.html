Nexo × DLT × Anduin — Integration Proposal

KO Certificate Flows with Anduin as Issuer: Flow of Funds, B-Book Operation & Data Sharing

Prepared by Anduin · September 2026 · Working draft — extends the KO Certificate Trading Flow (July 2026) and follows the structure of the Issuance MVP Flows Architecture (Flows A–D)

This document describes what changes when Anduin steps into the Issuer role of the Nexo × DLT × Issuer KO certificate setup. The flow of funds is unchanged from the July 2026 trading-flow document — Anduin simply is the Issuer: it holds Tangany Account 4, issues the certificates, and is the legal counterparty of every client cert trade and every hedge. What this document adds on top: (1) the operating model — Nexo runs the technical lift, Anduin only ever consumes data; (2) how the b-book works and who carries which risk; (3) exactly which data is shared, when, and through which mechanism, structured along Flows A–D of the Anduin issuance architecture.

Core assumption. Nexo intends / requests to run the technical lift on their side: quoting, execution, funding sync, re-marking, KO monitoring, hedge-order transmission and the full b-book operation all run on Nexo infrastructure. Anduin never operates real-time trading systems in this setup — Anduin is a data consumer and the system of record. Anduin's golden record, document set and archives must nonetheless be complete enough to stand up as the issuer's evidence trail, which is why the data-sharing obligations below are load-bearing, not nice-to-have.

2 · The b-book — how it works and who carries what Profit share

Legally there are two clean bilateral relationships. Technically there is one operator running everything. Keeping those two views separate is the whole trick.

2.1 Legal view

Two back-to-back positions

  1. Client ↔ Anduin: the client buys/sells KO certificates against Anduin (A4). Anduin is short the cert exposure.
  2. Anduin ↔ Nexo: for every fill, Anduin sends a hedge order to Nexo (legally) — a perp position that offsets the cert delta. After the hedge, Anduin is flat market risk: its residual exposure is counterparty exposure to Nexo, not market exposure.
  3. Nexo ↔ market: Nexo now holds the client flow risk and decides for itself whether to internalise it (b-book: keep the risk, earn the client's losses / pay the client's gains) or lay it off externally (a-book, e.g. ByBit) — entirely at Nexo's discretion, as in the trading-flow doc.

DLT's execution role & best execution

DLT executes client orders as proprietary trading (Eigenhandel) with back-to-back trades against Anduin. For German clients DLT acts instead under its Investment and Contract Brokerage licence (Anlage- und Abschlussvermittlung), brokering the trade between the client and Anduin. Since no other trading venue lists these tokens, executing back-to-back against Anduin's quotes is sufficient to satisfy best-execution requirements.

2.2 Technical view

Since Nexo runs the technical lift, the hedge order is technically generated and sent by Nexo, on behalf of Anduin, to Nexo-the-LP. Nexo is simultaneously the transmitting agent (acting for Anduin) and the receiving liquidity provider. Nexo then operates the b-book fully on Anduin's behalf: netting long/short client flow, deciding warehousing vs external hedging, managing the venue account.

For this to be defensible, the agency relationship must be evidenced in data: every hedge order must exist as a record that reads "Anduin instructs Nexo" (order ID, series, size, direction, timestamp, triggering client fill) and must be shared with Anduin — see the data-sharing matrix. Without that record, "legally Anduin's hedge order" is an assertion, not a fact.

3 · End-to-end data flow — the 14 steps

The single-page view of how data moves across the whole certificate lifecycle. Every step below is one concrete data movement (or the computation that triggers one); the numbering runs continuously from series creation to knock-out and carries through the master diagram. Flows A–D in the following sections give the full detail per phase — this section is the map.

Series creation & quoting start — Flow A detail in §4

  1. Nexo → Anduin: series creation request with the full parameter set — underlying, direction, KO/liquidation price, token count, maintenance buffer, ratio, funding spread, reference price source.
  2. Anduin → Nexo: creation confirmed — Anduin validates, builds the golden record, generates + publishes Final Terms and PRIIPs KID, mints to A4, and replies with series_id, token_address and the doc URLs.
  3. Underlying venue → Nexo: Nexo consumes the orderbook of the underlying venue (e.g. ByBit) and constructs the certificate book from it — on behalf of Anduin.
  4. Nexo → Users: Nexo streams firm cert prices to users (the price flow runs into the DLT AWS server, where spread / fees are added, before it reaches the user — see the quoting flow in §2.3).
  5. Nexo → Anduin (drop copy): every quote Nexo streams is drop-copied to Anduin's insert-only quote archive, delivered over SQS (only Anduin stores quotes — see §4).

At re-mark / funding (every 8h) — Flow B detail in §5

  1. Nexo (compute): Nexo calculates the new contract values from the venue's published funding rate — K' = K × (1 + f ± s), B' = K' × (1 ± buffer) — and re-marks its quoting.
  2. Nexo → Anduin (drop copy, instant): the complete level row (series_id, funding_ts, f, s, K', B') the moment it is committed — Anduin's levels endpoint is what the Final Terms reference.

User trades on price — Flow C detail in §6

  1. Nexo (execute): Nexo executes the client order against Anduin's A4 inventory at the pinned quote.
  2. Nexo → DLT and Anduin (drop copy, instant): execution details — trade ID, quote_id, series, side, size, price, client reference, timestamp — to DLT (SQS reporting rail) and to Anduin's trade ledger.
  3. Nexo → Nexo-the-LP: Nexo sends the hedge order to itself as liquidity provider — technically transmitted by Nexo, legally Anduin instructing Nexo (§2).
  4. Nexo → Anduin (drop copy, instant): the hedge order + fill record, flagged "on behalf of Anduin" and linked to the triggering trade ID — this record is what makes the agency construction real.

At knock-out — Flow D detail in §7

  1. Underlying venue → Nexo: Nexo observes the mark price of the underlying venue against the latest committed barrier — every reference tick.
  2. Nexo (halt): on touch, Nexo stops trading of the series immediately — quotes pulled, orders blocked, KO declared at the mark price reached.
  3. Nexo → Anduin (drop copy, instant): the full liquidation package — trigger tick + ±60s reference stream, level row in force, hedge unwind executions + VWAP, the liquidation/residual-value determination with all inputs, and payout records per holder (KO event also to DLT via SQS).

Reading the map

  • Direction of data: after step 2, everything flows from Nexo — Anduin is a data consumer and the system of record; DLT receives trade/KO events on its existing reporting rail (quotes go only to Anduin).
  • Drop copies are load-bearing: steps 5, 7, 9, 11 and 14 are what make Anduin's golden record, the KO defence and the b-book profit-share audit possible (§2.3, §8).
  • Latency classes: steps 7, 9, 11, 14 are instant push-on-commit; step 5 may be batched (1s batches) — see the data-sharing matrix in §8.

4 · Flow A — Series creation, documents & quoting start

Trigger. Nexo wants a new certificate series (new underlying, direction, or leverage band). Nexo requests creation from Anduin; Anduin runs issuance (golden record → docs → mint) and hands back everything Nexo needs to render and quote the product.

Sequence

  1. Series request — Nexo → Anduin API. Nexo requests creation of a series including: underlying + direction, KO / liquidation price (initial strike & barrier), number of tokens (max size), maintenance buffer (barrier distance), ratio, funding-spread, reference price source and hedge instrument. MVP note this MAY be a manual process initially (structurer form / ticket into Anduin's admin UI); the API is the target state. Either way the parameter set and the validation rules are identical.
  2. Anduin issuance kicks off. Anduin validates and creates the golden record (status draft), runs document creation (Final Terms rendered deterministically from the record + PRIIPs KID with SRI and performance scenarios), publishes both (status filed), then deploys and mints the full size to treasury in A4 (status live).
  3. Links back to Nexo. Anduin returns series_id, token_address, and the public URLs of Final Terms and PRIIPs KID — Nexo renders these in its UI (docs must be one click away from the order ticket; publication legally opens the offer and must precede any purchasable cert).
  4. Quoting commences — by Nexo, on behalf of Anduin. Once docs are published and the series is live, Nexo's pricing engine starts streaming quotes to users. Every quote Nexo provides on Anduin's behalf must be stored — by Anduin, and only by Anduin (Anduin's archive rule: if the issuer published it or computed money from it, it lands in an insert-only table). DLT does not need to store quotes. Mechanism: SQS, same pattern as the existing DLT reporting rail — quotes are batched (e.g. 1s COPY batches) onto a dedicated quote queue that Anduin consumes; the existing DLT queues carry trade events only, unchanged.

Flow A data shared with Anduin

  • Inbound to Anduin (request): full series parameter set (this is what Anduin builds the golden record from — Anduin validates, it does not blindly mint what is requested).
  • Outbound from Anduin: series identifiers + doc URLs (golden record fields Nexo needs for rendering and quoting: strike, barrier, ratio, tier sizes).
  • Continuous once live: every quote (Nexo → Anduin via SQS; DLT stores no quotes).

5 · Flow B — Funding sync & re-marking Instant sharing

Trigger. Every 8h at the hedge venue's published funding timestamp. Financing is funding pass-through into the strike (K' = K × (1 + f ± s)), barrier follows (B' = K' × (1 ± buffer)). No client cash flows, no doc amendments — Final Terms define the formula; current levels are published via endpoint only.

Sequence

  1. Nexo runs the sync. Nexo's lifecycle daemon detects the venue's published funding rate, computes the new strike and barrier per live series, and re-marks the certs (its pricing engine swaps in K' before the next quote).
  2. Data shared INSTANTLY with Anduin. On every adjustment, Nexo pushes the complete level row per series — (series_id, funding_ts, f, s, K_old, K_new, B_new) — to Anduin the moment it is committed. Not end-of-day, not batched: instant, because Anduin's published levels are what the Final Terms formula points at, and a gap between what Nexo quotes against and what Anduin publishes is a regulatory problem, not a cosmetic one.
  3. Anduin reflects it. Anduin appends to level_history and serves the authoritative levels endpoint (GET /series/:id/levels) that the docs reference and the website renders. Docs are never amended per adjustment.

Flow B guardrails

  • Idempotency: the push is keyed (series_id, funding_ts); re-delivery upserts the identical row, never double-applies.
  • Ordering & replay: if Anduin's ingestion is down over a funding ts, Nexo buffers and replays all missed events in order on reconnect. Anduin flags the affected series until levels catch up.
  • Restatements: if the venue restates a rate, Nexo pushes a correction row under the same key; Anduin appends the correction and auto-publishes a note on the levels endpoint.
  • Daily recon: Anduin recomputes each day's level chain from the venue's published rates (public data) and diffs against what Nexo pushed — pass-through must be provably clean since Anduin's docs promise a pure formula.

6 · Flow C — Trade, settlement & hedge

Trigger. Continuous. Clients hit Nexo's streamed quotes; Nexo executes against Anduin's inventory in A4; cash and cert legs settle per the trading-flow doc; every fill produces a hedge record and feeds the b-book.

Sequence

  1. Execution by Nexo. Client order → UI → OCS → fill against A4 inventory at the pinned quote. Nexo shares execution details with Anduin — instantly, per fill — so Anduin reflects it in the golden record's trade ledger: trade ID, referenced quote_id, series, side, size, price, client reference, timestamp, and the resulting outstanding. Outstanding per series is derived from these events, never overwritten — Anduin cannot compute its own exposure, RV obligations, or the profit-share check without a complete fill stream.
  2. Settlement — unchanged, as outlined in the trading-flow doc. German: cash directly A1 ↔ A4; non-German: A1 → A3 → A4 (and back on sale) with A3 holding funds only for a second; cert legs directly A4 ↔ A1; fee (0.06%) to A3; T+0 book entries, T+1 physical net; SQS events to DLT per trade. A4 is Anduin's account, so Anduin additionally receives the settlement confirmations (Tangany account statements / T+1 net files) and reconciles them against the fill stream daily.
  3. Hedge — legal vs technical. For every fill, Anduin sends the hedge order to Nexo (legally), while technically the hedge order is generated and sent by Nexo on behalf of Anduin to Nexo-the-LP. The hedge record (order + fill: instrument, size, direction, price, timestamp, triggering trade ID, "on behalf of Anduin" flag) is pushed to Anduin instantly — this record is what makes the agency construction real.
  4. B-book operation. Nexo operates the b-book fully on Anduin's behalf: netting client flow, warehousing or externally hedging at its own discretion. B-book profits are shared — Nexo delivers a periodic P&L statement (daily position/PnL snapshot + monthly settlement statement proposed); Anduin verifies it against the quote/fill/hedge streams it already holds.

What changes when a German client is involved

Not much in the flow itself — the difference is who Anduin must be able to identify. For non-German clients Anduin does not hold the end-client relationship: it only needs to know which trades / orders DLT made (a DLT-level reference is sufficient). For German clients Anduin holds the client relationship, so the client must be identified and reflected in the golden record — the SQS fill messages for German clients therefore contain client information; for non-German clients they carry the DLT reference only.

7 · Flow D — Knock-out & settlement

Trigger. The reference price touches the barrier. Because Nexo provides the trading, Nexo observes the mark prices of the underlying venue, declares the KO and halts trading immediately. It then unwinds the hedge on behalf of Anduin and pays back the user. Everything is shared with Anduin — this is the flow where the issuer's legal risk concentrates, so evidence completeness is the binding requirement.

Sequence

  1. Detection & declaration (Nexo). Nexo monitors every reference tick (the mark price source named in the Final Terms) against the latest committed barrier. On touch: KO declared, trading halted immediately — quotes pulled, orders blocked for the series.
  2. Evidence first → shared with Anduin. The trigger tick plus the surrounding ±60s of the reference stream and the level row in force are captured and pushed to Anduin instantly. The KO is only defensible if the issuer holds the exact market data acted on — Anduin archives this before anything else proceeds.
  3. Hedge unwind (Nexo, on behalf of Anduin). Nexo unwinds the series' hedge contribution; every unwind execution is shared with Anduin — the unwind VWAP feeds the residual value.
  4. Residual value & payout (Nexo pays the user). RV is computed per Final Terms against the strike (long: RV = max(0, (unwind_VWAP − K) × ratio − costs)). Nexo pays the residual value back to the user: cash leg from A4 per the trading-flow doc (German: A4 → A1 direct; non-German: A4 → A3 → A1), certs debited from A1, returned to A4 and cancelled. RV = 0 follows the identical process minus the cash transfer — the zero-RV determination with its inputs is itself an artifact Anduin must receive.
  5. All information shared with Anduin. Trigger evidence, halt timestamp, unwind executions + VWAP, RV calculation with all inputs, payout records per holder, and the final token cancellation. Anduin closes the series in the golden record (knocked_out → settled), publishes the KO notice on its docs/levels pages (trigger ts, trigger price, RV determination, payment date), retires the treasury tokens, and archives the full package (≥7y retention, docs stay retrievable at their URLs).

Flow D non-negotiables (issuer's legal exposure)

  • Evidence before action reaches Anduin's archive. If Nexo declares a KO and Anduin never receives the tick evidence, Anduin cannot defend the termination against a holder dispute. The evidence push is part of the KO procedure, not an after-the-fact report.
  • KO during Nexo downtime is still a KO — trigger = first qualifying tick; Nexo must replay after reconnect and the declaration backdates to the touch.
  • RV = 0 produces the same artifact set. The zero-RV determination with inputs is the defence when a holder disputes receiving nothing.
  • No early action. Nothing is unwound pre-touch; pre-KO only quoting changes (ask pulled near the barrier, bid stays up).

Design principle

Nexo operates, Anduin can always reconstruct. Every number Anduin publishes (levels), owes (RV payouts), or earns (spread, profit share) must be recomputable from the streams above without asking Nexo. If a data item fails that test, its latency or granularity is wrong.
