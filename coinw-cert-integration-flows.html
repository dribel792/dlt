CoinW × Black Manta × DLT × Anduin — Integration Proposal

Securitized Perps on CoinW: Technical Integration Flow & Flow of Funds

Prepared by Anduin · October 2026 · Working draft v0.1 — companion to the CoinW Listing Primer; adapts the KO Certificate Trading Flow (Nexo model, July 2026) to the CoinW relationship

This document answers the two questions from the kick-off: what does the flow of funds look like, and what does the integration look like on a technical level — who does what in this relationship. It adapts the proven Nexo-model flow architecture to the CoinW chain. Names and payload shapes are indicative; the goal is to agree the shape.

The chain in one line

User → CoinW UI (tied agent of Black Manta) → RTO → DLT (broker) → STP → Anduin (issuer) → STP → MM (CoinW MM or an Anduin MM). Anduin consumes executable books on the certificates directly from the MM and fills every client order straight-through against them — no order is ever worked, warehoused or discretionarily priced anywhere in the chain.

1 · Operating model — who does what

One structural difference vs the Nexo setup is worth stating up front: in the Nexo model the distributor ran the technical lift (quoting, lifecycle, KO monitoring) and the issuer was a data consumer. In the CoinW setup this flips: Anduin runs the issuer systems — quoting off the MM's books, funding sync, KO monitoring, document generation — while CoinW runs only the frontend and DLT routes and reports. The MM is the liquidity source and risk taker.

Why this split is clean

  • CoinW never touches a security's pricing or risk — it transmits orders as tied agent and renders feeds. Minimal lift, minimal regulatory surface.
  • DLT never holds risk — every execution is back-to-back, booked T+0, reported.
  • Anduin never holds market risk — every fill is STP'd to the MM the moment it happens; the issuer's residual exposure is counterparty exposure to the MM, not market exposure.
  • The MM is the single point where market risk lives — which is exactly where it belongs, and (if CoinW MM) exactly where CoinW wants it.

2 · The quote chain and the order chain

2.1 Quoting — continuous, MM-driven

The MM streams executable two-way books on each live certificate series directly to Anduin. Anduin's quoting engine validates each book against its own reference calculation (the Final Terms formula on the current strike/barrier levels — the issuer must never quote off stale or deviating levels), adds the issuer spread, and streams firm quotes to DLT; DLT forwards them to the CoinW UI, where the trading fee is displayed per the fee schedule. Every quote Anduin publishes lands in its insert-only quote archive (regulatory record + price-pinning evidence).

2.2 Execution — RTO → STP → STP, no discretion anywhere

The order chain is fully straight-through: CoinW EU receives the user's order and transmits it (RTO) to DLT; DLT executes back-to-back against Anduin's firm quote (STP); Anduin fills from its inventory and simultaneously STPs the offsetting transaction to the MM. Three hops, zero discretion, seconds end-to-end.

3 · Lifecycle flows

3.1 Flow A — Series creation, documents & quoting start

Trigger. A new series is needed (new underlying, direction or leverage band) — requested by CoinW via DLT, or created proactively by Anduin (e.g. the replacement series after a knock-out). Anduin runs issuance end-to-end.

Sequence

  1. Series request. Parameter set: underlying + identifiers, direction, KO/liquidation price (initial strike & barrier), token count, maintenance buffer, ratio, funding spread, reference price source. MVP note may be manual (structurer form) initially; API is target state.
  2. Anduin issuance. Validate → golden record → generate Final Terms + PRIIPs KID → publish (publication legally opens the offer) → mint to treasury → series live.
  3. Links down the chain. Anduin returns series_id, token_address and the public Final Terms / KID URLs; CoinW renders the doc links in the UI (KID one click from the order ticket — hard regulatory requirement).
  4. Quoting commences. The MM starts streaming books on the new series to Anduin; Anduin starts publishing firm quotes (§2.1).

3.2 Flow B — Funding sync & re-marking Anduin computes

Trigger. Every 8h at the reference venue's published funding timestamp. Financing is funding pass-through into the strike (K' = K × (1 + f ± s)), barrier follows (B' = K' × (1 ± buffer)). No client cash flows, no doc amendments — the Final Terms define the formula; current levels are published via Anduin's levels endpoint.

Sequence

  1. Anduin's lifecycle daemon detects the venue's published funding rate, computes the new strike and barrier per live series, appends level_history (idempotent on series_id + funding_ts) and serves the authoritative levels endpoint the Final Terms reference.
  2. Push to the MM — the MM re-marks its books on the new levels before its next quote. A deviation between the MM's quoted strike and Anduin's published levels trips an automated discrepancy alert and pulls the quote stream.
  3. Push to DLT / CoinW — the UI updates the liq. price line on the chart and the position cards from the same levels feed.

3.3 Flow C — Trade, settlement & MM leg

Execution per §2.2. Settlement is DvP across the custodian accounts (§4). For every client fill the offsetting MM fill exists as a linked record (triggering trade ID ↔ MM fill ID) in Anduin's ledger — this is what proves the issuer is flat and what any profit-share arrangement with the MM is audited against. DLT receives the trade event on its reporting rail at T+0.

3.4 Flow D — Knock-out

Trigger. The reference price touches the barrier. Anduin monitors every reference tick, declares the KO and halts quoting immediately — this is the flow where the issuer's legal risk concentrates, so the evidence trail is the binding requirement.

Sequence

  1. Detection & declaration (Anduin). On touch: KO declared, quotes pulled, orders blocked for the series. The trigger tick + ±60s of the reference stream + the level row in force are archived first — the KO defence file.
  2. Unwind vs the MM. Anduin closes the series' offsetting position against the MM; the unwind VWAP feeds the residual value.
  3. Residual value & payout. RV per Final Terms (RV = max(0, (unwind_VWAP − K) × ratio − costs), long; short mirrored). USDC paid to holders of record via the custodian (§4); certs debited, returned and cancelled. RV = 0 produces the identical artifact set minus the cash leg.
  4. Notices. KO event to DLT (reporting) and to CoinW (UI shows the liquidation screen from the Listing Primer — no liquidation fee, no negative balance, residual paid automatically); replacement series issued immediately so the UI can offer the new 30×.

4 · Flow of funds

Everything sits at one EU custodian (MiCAR-regulated; entity to be confirmed with CoinW). Each party has an account at that same custodian: User Account · CoinW Account · DLT Account · Anduin Account. Trading moves book entries between those accounts; physical assets never leave the custodian's perimeter. The MM relationship (Anduin ↔ MM) settles outside the custodian under the bilateral MM agreement — it never touches client accounts.

4.2 Sell / knock-out — the same rails in reverse

  • Sell: certs move User Account → Anduin Account (returned and cancelled), cash moves Anduin Account → User Account, fee User Account → DLT Account — all T+0 book entries at the same custodian; Anduin closes the matching position vs the MM.
  • Knock-out: identical to a sell with proceeds = residual value (possibly zero), per Flow D. No liquidation fee, no negative balance.
  • Physical settlement: the T+0 entries are the legal record; physical movements between custodian accounts net out on a T+1 bulk basis (net delta vs prior state), as in the running Nexo setup.
