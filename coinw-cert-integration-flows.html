CoinW × Black Manta × DLT × Anduin — Integration Proposal

Securitized Perps on CoinW: Technical Integration Flow & Flow of Funds

Prepared by Anduin · October 2026 · Working draft v0.2 — companion to the CoinW Listing Primer; adapts the KO Certificate Trading Flow (Nexo model, July 2026) to the CoinW relationship

This document answers the two questions from the kick-off: what does the integration look like on a technical level — who does what in this relationship — and what does the flow of funds look like. Part A presents the two options for pricing-data provision and execution (Flow 1: perp books in, Anduin builds the cert book · Flow 2: cert books in, full STP). Part B presents the flow of funds with ZBX (CoinW's EU MiCAR partner) as custodian. Names and payload shapes are indicative; the goal is to agree the shape.

The chain in one line

User → CoinW UI / API (frontend under a tied-agent relationship with Black Manta) → RTO/STP → DLT (broker, collects the fee) → STP/prop → Anduin (issuer) → hedge or STP → MM (CoinW MM or an Anduin MM). There is no OCS in this setup — the order chain is three direct hops, no order is worked or warehoused anywhere, and every hop runs on that party's own servers (co-location in one datacenter possible for latency).

A1 · Actors — who does what

PartyRole & responsibilityWhat they run technically
CoinW (frontend) Operates the client-facing UI / API under a tied-agent relationship with Black Manta. Owns the user relationship and order entry; RTOs / STPs orders to DLT. No execution, no pricing, no risk. UI/API (perp-style surface per the Listing Primer), order transmission to DLT, rendering of the quote / levels / docs feeds.
CoinW Tied Agent Entity The CoinW entity registered as tied agent of Black Manta — the legal face of the distribution. Holds an account at ZBX and receives its share of the trading fee from DLT. No real-time systems in the flow.
Black Manta (Haftungsdach) The MiFID-licensed securities house whose tied-agent umbrella makes CoinW's retail order transmission in a security lawful. Always named together with DLT — the two come as one unit. No real-time systems; compliance reporting from DLT as agreed bilaterally.
DLT (broker) Executing broker. Consumes Anduin's quote stream and serves it to the CoinW UI; receives the RTO'd orders and passes them to Anduin as STP / prop (back-to-back, riskless principal), adding its spread or fee at this hop. Records every cert trade at T+0; regulatory reporting counterparty; collects the trading fee and credits the agreed share to the CoinW Tied Agent Entity. Since no other venue lists these tokens, back-to-back execution against the issuer satisfies best execution. Market-data redistribution (WS/FIX in from Anduin, stream out to CoinW), order routing, T+0 securities trade book, reporting rail, fee accounting.
Anduin (issuer) Issuer of record. Owns the golden record per series, generates and publishes Final Terms + PRIIPs KID, mints the tokens, is the counterparty of every client cert trade, runs the 8h funding sync (strike/barrier), monitors and declares knock-outs, computes residual values. Flow 1: consumes perp books from the MM and constructs the cert books itself; hedges every fill with a perp trade on the MM. Flow 2: consumes cert books from the MM and STPs every order to the MM. Flat market risk in both. Registry + doc-gen, token contracts, golden record DB, pricing/booking engine (Flow 1) or STP router (Flow 2), lifecycle daemon (funding + KO), WS/FIX market-data service to DLT, public levels/docs endpoints, append-only archive.
MM (CoinW MM or an Anduin MM) Liquidity provider to Anduin. Flow 1: streams perp books (WS/FIX) and takes Anduin's perp hedge trades. Flow 2: streams cert books incl. the underlying markets and takes the STP'd cert orders directly. Manages the resulting market risk at its own discretion. When the MM is CoinW MM, the flow economics stay inside CoinW — the internalization story from the Listing Primer. Pricing engine (perps or certs), WS/FIX market-data + execution API towards Anduin, own hedging infrastructure, margin account management (see Part B).
ZBX (= CoinW EU, MiCAR partner · custodian) CoinW's EU MiCAR partner and the custodian of the assets. User, DLT, Anduin and the CoinW Tied Agent Entity all hold accounts at ZBX. All client-side money and token movements are book entries between ZBX accounts; assets never leave the custody perimeter. Account/booking API, "leverage trading" assignment flags, statements, rebalancing files.

A2 · Pricing & execution — two options

Both options use the same order chain (CoinW → DLT → Anduin) and the same fee logic (DLT adds and collects). They differ in what the MM streams and therefore in who prices the certificate and who carries which risk.

Flow 1 — MM streams perp books Anduin pricesFlow 2 — MM streams cert books MM prices
Market data in Perp books of the reference markets, WS/FIX from the MM to Anduin. Cert books (incl. underlying markets), WS/FIX from the MM to Anduin.
Cert book construction Anduin derives the cert book from the perp book via the Final Terms formula on current levels. MM constructs the cert book, keyed to Anduin's published levels; Anduin validates and passes through.
Execution leg to the MM Perp hedge trade per client fill; MM confirms the perp execution price; Anduin derives and confirms the cert execution price to DLT. Cert order STP'd to the MM; MM confirms the cert execution price directly.
Anduin's residual risk Flat post-hedge; carries the quote-to-hedge latency/slippage gap (see A5). Fully riskless — instrument-level back-to-back per fill.
MM requirements Any perp MM works — no securities knowledge needed. MM must price and deal the security itself (bilateral professional-counterparty dealing under the MM agreement).
Who can be the MM CoinW MM or an Anduin MM — the interfaces are identical either way.

Hosting

All of this activity can be hosted in the same datacenter for latency optimization — but every hop must run through the respective party's own servers (CoinW's order gateway, DLT's broker stack, Anduin's issuer stack, the MM's engine). Co-location compresses latency; it does not collapse the chain into one operator — each entity demonstrably operates its own system.

B · Flow of funds — ZBX as custodian

CoinW works with ZBX as their MiCAR partner (ZBX = CoinW EU), which is the custodian of the assets. User, DLT, Anduin and the CoinW Tied Agent Entity each hold an account at ZBX. Client-side cash and token movements are book entries between these accounts. The margin relationship towards the MM (CoinW MM) runs alongside it and is trued up by a periodic rebalancing (T+1 as the working example).

B2 · Phase 1 — Setup & pre-trade (before any order)

Sequence

  1. Deposit. The user funds their account at ZBX (User Account).
  2. Assignment to "leverage trading". The user assigns part of their ZBX balance to leverage trading — a book-only earmark inside custody, like funding the futures wallet today.
  3. Assignment info → DLT. The assignment (amount, client reference, timestamps) is shared with DLT, so DLT can perform POSOF checks (proof of source of funds) where needed before accepting order flow against that balance.
  4. Mirroring on CoinW MM. All funds the user credits to leverage trading are mirrored by CoinW on CoinW MM as available margin / collateral for Anduin — i.e. CoinW makes equivalent margin capacity available on the MM so Anduin's hedge/STP leg can execute instantly when the user trades, without waiting for settlement. The user's actual assets stay in ZBX custody throughout (see B5 — this mirror is capacity, not a movement of client assets).

B3 · Phase 2 — Purchase (worked example: 10 certs à 700 USDC, fee 0.06%)

Sequence

  1. User buys 10 certs via the CoinW UI; the order runs the chain of Part A (CoinW RTO → DLT STP/prop → Anduin → MM).
  2. Cash leg: at the time of purchase the user's funds are credited to DLT including the fee (7,000 + 4.20 USDC), then credited onward to Anduin without the fee (7,000 USDC). T+0 book entries at ZBX.
  3. Fee split: DLT retains its share and credits the agreed part of the fee to the CoinW Tied Agent Entity's ZBX account.
  4. Security leg (DvP): 10 certs move Anduin Account → User Account.
  5. Pledge: Anduin pledges the received funds to CoinW — this is the collateral backing the margin capacity CoinW mirrored on CoinW MM in Phase 1 (utilized portion).
  6. MM leg: Anduin's hedge (Flow 1) or STP'd cert order (Flow 2) executes on CoinW MM against that margin.

B4 · Phase 3 — T+1 rebalancing of realized PnL

Sequence (T+1 as the working example — cadence is a parameter)

  1. Over the day, client sells / knock-outs realize PnL on Anduin's positions at CoinW MM.
  2. Positive realized PnL on CoinW MM is moved back to ZBX (CoinW MM → Anduin Account @ ZBX) — from there it funds client sale proceeds / KO residual payouts.
  3. Negative realized PnL is topped up in the other direction (Anduin Account @ ZBX → CoinW MM), keeping the margin account whole.
  4. The mirrored margin capacity is re-trued to the current sum of user leverage-trading assignments; Anduin's pledge is re-trued to utilized positions.

C · Design notes & points to pin down

PointNote / proposal
One rule for Anduin's margin at CoinW MM Two things point at the same margin account: the mirror (sized by user leverage-trading assignments) and Anduin's pledge of received trade funds. Proposal: the mirror is unfunded capacity (a limit, sized by assignments) and the pledge is the funding of the utilized portion; the T+1 rebalance trues both up. Written this way there is no double-counting and no unsecured gap — but it must be fixed in the MM/margin agreement, not left implicit.
Client-asset segregation at the mirror The user's assigned funds remain client assets in ZBX custody. The mirror on CoinW MM is CoinW-side capacity, not a movement or re-use of client assets — the wording in all client-facing and regulatory material must keep this clean (MiCAR segregation).
Firm quotes vs hedge-derived pricing (Flow 1) In Flow 1 the cert execution price is confirmed after the perp hedge fills — i.e. hedge slippage passes to the client (market-order semantics). Alternative: Anduin quotes firm with a TTL and carries the quote-to-hedge gap itself. Decision needed — it drives best-execution wording and the UI's order types. Flow 2 avoids the question (the MM's cert fill is the price).
Intraday exposure window T+1 rebalancing means up to a day of realized PnL sits as bilateral exposure between Anduin and CoinW MM (both directions). Propose an intraday top-up trigger above a threshold move, and the cadence itself as a parameter (T+1 is the working example).
Flow 1 vs Flow 2 — MM capability Flow 1 works with any perp MM (no securities involvement). Flow 2 requires the MM to price and deal the security itself bilaterally — cleaner risk transfer for Anduin, higher bar for the MM. This is effectively the selection criterion between the two flows and between CoinW MM vs an Anduin MM.
Payout funding at KO / sale Sale proceeds and KO residuals are paid from Anduin's ZBX account, but the offsetting value sits at CoinW MM until rebalance. Anduin's ZBX account therefore needs a working-capital buffer sized to one rebalance period of expected outflows (or the rebalance runs intraday on large KO events).
Fee split & spread placement Fee collected by DLT, share to the CoinW Tied Agent Entity — split % is a commercial term. DLT's spread-or-fee at its hop and Anduin's issuer spread must be set jointly so the all-in client price stays competitive with the perp it mirrors.
