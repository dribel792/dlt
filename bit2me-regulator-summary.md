# Tokenised Knock-Out Certificates — Programme Summary

**Prepared for:** Bit2Me — for discussion with the Spanish regulator (CNMV)
**Date:** August 2026
**Status:** Informational summary — programme in structuring; prospectus approval pending

---

## 1. Purpose of this document

This document summarises a structured-products programme under which **DLT Finance**, a German BaFin-regulated financial institution, intends to distribute tokenised **Perpetual Knock-Out Certificates** to clients of Bit2Me. It describes the business model, the legal nature of the instrument, and the investor-protection framework, to support an early, transparent dialogue with the CNMV.

## 2. The product in one paragraph

The Perpetual Knock-Out Certificate is an **open-ended, leveraged transferable security** that gives investors economic exposure to an underlying asset (crypto-assets such as BTC/ETH, precious metals, equities/indices, commodities) with a pre-defined **knock-out barrier**. If the reference price touches the barrier, the certificate terminates automatically and any residual value is paid out. The economics are the familiar economics of leverage certificates (turbos / knock-outs) that have been distributed to European retail investors for two decades — the innovation is in the **issuance format** (a security token on a public blockchain) and in the **24/7 tradability** this enables, mirroring the always-open nature of the underlying crypto markets.

Key investor-relevant property: **losses are strictly limited to the amount invested.** There are no margin calls, no negative balances, and no debt obligation of any kind on the investor — a structurally safer profile than CFDs or margin trading.

## 3. Legal nature of the instrument

- **Classification:** Transferable security under **MiFID II Art. 4(1)(44)** — a securitised derivative comparable to the knock-out certificates listed on regulated European venues. It is **not** a CFD, not an OTC derivative, and not a crypto-asset under MiCA (financial instruments are expressly excluded from MiCA's scope, Art. 2(4)(a)).
- **Prospectus:** Issued under an **EU Prospectus Regulation (2017/1129) base prospectus** with per-series Final Terms. Approval will be sought from an EEA competent authority, with **passporting into Spain** so the offer to Spanish retail investors is fully prospectus-covered.
- **PRIIPs:** A **Key Information Document (KID)** in Spanish is produced for every series (Category 3; expected SRI 6–7 depending on leverage), with the standard cost, risk and performance-scenario disclosures.
- **Issuer:** A dedicated special-purpose issuance vehicle whose sole business is issuing the certificates and hedging its obligations. Final issuer jurisdiction is being settled with prospectus counsel.

## 4. The token format

Each certificate is issued natively as a **security token** — a DLT-based representation of the transferable security, following the pattern already established in the European market by tokenised money-market and equity instruments (e.g., the Ondo and xStocks issuance standards). Important design constraints:

- The token is **custodizable by an EEA qualified crypto-custodian** (German Kryptoverwahrgeschäft-licensed institutions such as Tangany or Bankhaus von der Heydt). Institutional-grade custody is a non-negotiable programme requirement.
- **KYC/AML at primary issuance** is performed by the regulated distributor; the token remains a registered transferable security throughout its life.
- The blockchain serves as the **register and settlement layer** — it does not change the legal nature of the instrument, which remains a security governed by its terms and the prospectus.

## 5. Business model and distribution chain

1. **Issuer (SPV)** issues each certificate series under the base prospectus and immediately **hedges its exposure back-to-back** on liquid reference markets. The issuer runs a market-neutral book: it takes no directional risk against its investors, and its revenue derives from transparent product spreads and financing charges disclosed in the KID.
2. **DLT Finance (BaFin-regulated)** acts as the MiFID distributor and execution intermediary: client onboarding, appropriateness assessment for complex instruments, order handling, best execution, and ongoing MiFID II conduct-of-business compliance.
3. **Bit2Me** provides its clients access to the product through its platform, with DLT Finance as the regulated counterparty for the MiFID product. Spanish clients therefore face a regulated EU investment firm, purchasing a prospectus-backed security.

## 6. Investor protection summary

- Prospectus-approved, EEA-passported transferable security — not an unregulated derivative.
- Spanish-language PRIIPs KID per series; target-market definition and appropriateness testing under MiFID II product governance.
- Maximum loss capped at the invested amount; automatic knock-out removes the possibility of debt.
- Market-neutral, fully hedged issuer; no conflict of interest between issuer and investor P&L.
- Qualified, licensed custody of the tokens; continuous pricing against transparent, published reference prices defined in the Final Terms.

## 7. What is genuinely new — and what is not

**Not new:** the payoff (knock-out certificates are a mature, retail-distributed European product class), the regulatory wrapper (Prospectus Regulation + PRIIPs + MiFID II), or the distribution model (regulated intermediary to retail).

**New:** the instrument is issued and settled as a security token, which enables 24/7 issuance, transfer and redemption in step with the underlying crypto markets, near-instant settlement, and full auditability of the register — while keeping the instrument inside the existing securities-law perimeter.

We believe this combination — a familiar, well-understood retail product delivered through modern settlement technology under the full EU securities framework — is exactly the direction European regulators have encouraged through the DLT Pilot Regime and MiCA's clear demarcation of financial instruments, and we welcome the CNMV's questions and guidance.
