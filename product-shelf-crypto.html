DLT Finance · Product 1 · How It Works

Securitised perps — a perp's economics, delivered as a security

DLT Finance
Payoff · Open-end long certificateCertificate value vs. underlying
Leverage is set by the distance to the barrier — not by a margin ratio. Loss is bounded by construction: no margin call, no negative balance.
The mechanics, in one line each
  • The user buys a certificate, not a position. They pay the premium in full — that is the entire outlay and the entire risk.
  • The certificate tracks the underlying one for one, multiplied by the ratio. Value = (price − barrier) × ratio.
  • The carry replaces funding. A daily financing rate is rolled into the barrier, so the economics match a perp without a funding transfer.
  • If the barrier is touched, it knocks out — the position closes at zero, exactly where a perp would have been liquidated.
  • DLT hedges the exposure on the venue in the background. Your user never sees it.

DLT Finance · Product 2 · Leverage ETPs

Turbos and mini futures — same machinery, different barrier

DLT Finance
Leverage is a dial, not a settingbuffer to barrier → leverage
One instrument family covers 3× to 30×. The user picks the leverage; we issue the strike that produces it.
Turbo / knock-out certificate
  • Barrier sits at the strike. Touch it and the certificate expires worthless — the equivalent of a liquidation.
  • Highest leverage on the shelf, because the buffer is thinnest. 3× to 30×, long or short.
  • Open-end. No expiry date, no roll — the user holds it as long as the barrier holds.
Mini future
  • Stop-loss sits above the strike. On a knock-out the residual value between the two is paid back — the user does not lose the whole premium.
  • Softer product for a broader audience. 2× to 20×, with a visible safety gap.
  • The strike and stop adjust daily with the financing rate, so the leverage stays roughly where the user bought it.
What you get from listing them
  • A leverage ladder, not a single product. The same user trades 5× on Monday and 30× on Friday without leaving your app.
  • Every underlying we price. Crypto, equities, indices, metals and commodities — identical mechanics on all of them.
  • No margin infrastructure. Fully-paid securities, settled in cash or USDC.
  • The ticket looks like a perp ticket. Direction, size, leverage — the user never has to learn what a strike is.

DLT Finance · Product 3 · Leverage ETPs

Factor certificates — constant leverage, never knocked out

DLT Finance
3× factor vs. underlying · a choppy weekindexed to 100
Leverage resets every day, so returns compound. In a trend that works for the holder; in a chop it works against them. This is the one thing to explain in the UI.
How it works
  • A fixed multiple of the daily move — ±2× to ±10×, long or short. The factor never changes.
  • The exposure is rebalanced each day back to the stated factor. That is what keeps the leverage constant.
  • No barrier, no knock-out, no expiry. A 5× factor cannot be stopped out by an intraday wick — the value falls, but the position survives.
  • Returns compound over multiple days, so a factor certificate is not a buy-and-hold product. It is a momentum tool.
