DLT Finance · Structured Products

Structured products — one recipe, any payoff you want

DLT Finance
The whole category in four sentences

A structured product is a package. Most of the money goes somewhere safe that earns interest. A small slice buys an option. The safe part guarantees the capital comes back; the option part is the upside. Change the mix and you change the payoff — that is the entire idea.

~90% kept safe, earning interestthis is what guarantees the capital comes back
~10%buys upside
Capital protectionThe bonus
Live
Shark fin
Capital back in full, plus a bonus that grows the closer the underlying ends to a barrier. Break the barrier and the bonus is lost, never the capital.
For the user who says
"I want yield, but I cannot lose the principal."
Live
Covered call
Premium income on a coin the user already holds, paid up front, in exchange for capping the upside above a strike.
For the user who says
"My BTC is just sitting there earning nothing."
Live
Zero-interest certificate
Full capital protection, no coupon, and participation in the upside above a strike. The cleanest entry into a volatile asset.
For the user who says
"I want crypto exposure without the drawdown."
Live
Downside-protected note
Capital back at maturity plus a capped return over six to thirty-six months. The most conservative product on the shelf.
For the user who says
"Beat my savings account, take no risk with the principal."
Live
Barrier reverse convertible
A high fixed coupon paid whatever happens. Capital is only at risk if the underlying breaches a barrier well below spot.
For the user who says
"Pay me a real coupon; I can live with a deep drawdown."
Live
Dual-currency deposit
Enhanced yield on a short deposit, repaid in either of two assets depending on where the price finishes.
For the user who says
"I am happy to own either asset at that price."
Why it matters to you

This is the only family that holds a deposit in place for weeks or months — a balance that cannot leave for a competitor mid-term — and it reaches the users who will never open a leverage ticket. All six run on the same pricing and hedging engine — and they are examples, not a menu: tell us the payoff and we build it.
