KO Certificate Trading Flow: End-to-End with Issuer Hedging (USDC)

Prepared by Nexo · July 2026 · Working draft — amends the Futures Trading Flow (May 2026) for certificate trading

This document describes the money-flow and execution model for Nexo × DLT KO certificate (turbo) trading. Between User, App UI, OCS and the Issuer, only KO certificates are traded: every client order is a purchase or sale of KO certificates executed against the Issuer. The Issuer then creates hedge orders against Nexo — entering into a perp position with Nexo — while Nexo does not have to hedge externally (e.g. ByBit) and can decide for itself.

Custody model. All parties are part of the Tangany setup — the Issuer onboards with Tangany as well and receives Tangany Account 4 (Issuer). Client collateral physically sits in Tangany Account 1 (User account) with the internal sub-split (1.1) Free (transit / non-earmarked) and (1.2) Futures (trading-wallet portion) unchanged. Account 4 is the source of the certificates issued and the source for the hedging positions against Nexo.

Earn opt-in / opt-out applies only to Transfer to Futures and Transfer from Futures. All other transactions (cert purchases/sales, fees, knock-out redemptions) are opt-in/out agnostic because funds remain in Account 1 throughout the trade lifecycle.

Pledge model (amended). Pledging always happens before buying. Funds can no longer be pledged to Nexo. Non-German clients pledge funds only to DLT, and DLT MAY pledge them onward to the Issuer. German clients (onboarded with the Issuer) pledge only to the Issuer — no pledge to DLT. As before, pledges are a legal / book-entry arrangement only — no physical fund movement between Tangany accounts for the pledged amount.

T+0 vs T+1 (amended). DLT records each purchase of security tokens and any ledger movement as an individual book entry at T+0. The Issuer records (a) any ledger movement of the tokens it issues and (b) each hedge trade it makes against Nexo. Physical net settlement between Tangany accounts remains on a T+1 bulk basis (net delta vs prior state) on the operational layer.

German vs non-German clients

For German clients only, each client is onboarded with the Issuer in addition to the Tangany onboarding. All actors in this document (User, Nexo, DLT, Issuer) are onboarded with Tangany. The two client groups settle and pledge differently:

  • German clients: cert cash legs move directly A1 ↔ A4; the pledge goes only to the Issuer.
  • Non-German clients: cert cash legs always move through A3 first — A1 → A3 → A4 on purchase, A4 → A3 → A1 on sale — even though the funds sit in A3 only for a second; the pledge goes only to DLT (DLT MAY pledge onward to the Issuer).
  • Pledging always happens before buying. Trading fees go to A3 in both cases; the security (cert) legs settle directly A4 ↔ A1 in both cases.

What changed vs the perp flow

  • Instrument: clients trade KO certificates (securities), not perps. The perp only exists as the Issuer's hedge against Nexo.
  • New actor / account: Issuer with Tangany Account 4 — source of issued certs and of the hedge positions vs Nexo.
  • Execution: User → App UI → OCS → Issuer (certs only). Issuer → Nexo (perp hedge). Nexo may hedge externally at its own discretion.
  • Funding: positive / negative funding does not exist — it is baked into the certificate terms (8h strike/barrier adjustment at the hedge venue’s funding timestamps). No client-side session settlement either. The trading fee remains.
  • Pledge: always before buying. Non-German: User → DLT only (DLT MAY pledge onward to the Issuer). German: User → Issuer only. Never to Nexo.
  • German vs non-German routing: German clients settle cert cash legs directly A1 ↔ A4; non-German clients always route them through A3 first (A1 → A3 → A4 / A4 → A3 → A1), even if A3 holds the funds only for a second.
  • Liquidation → Knock-out: instead of margin liquidation, the certificate terminates when spot touches the barrier; loss is bounded to the premium paid.

Canonical recipes

  • Pledge (always before buying): German clients pledge only to the Issuer; non-German clients pledge only to DLT (DLT MAY pledge onward to the Issuer). Book-entry only.
  • Buy certs (client pays): internal (1.2) → (1.1) → T+0 cash leg: German Debit USDC A1 → Credit A4 (direct); non-German Debit A1 → Credit A3 → immediately Debit A3 → Credit A4 → T+0 book: Debit certs A4 → Credit A1 → internal (1.1) → (1.2) → SQS to DLT. Issuer records the token ledger movement.
  • Sell certs / KO redemption (client receives): internal certs (1.2) → (1.1) → T+0 book: Debit certs A1 → Credit A4 (cancelled) → T+0 cash leg: German Debit USDC A4 → Credit A1 (direct); non-German Debit A4 → Credit A3 → immediately Debit A3 → Credit A1 → internal (1.1) → (1.2) → SQS to DLT.
  • Hedge (Issuer-side): for every client cert fill the Issuer opens/closes a perp position against Nexo out of A4. Never touches client accounts. Nexo may hedge externally at its own discretion.
  • Trading fees: always settled in USDC to A3, even when collateral is BTC or other assets. 0.06% of notional.
  • No funding / no session settlement: financing is baked into the certificate terms — there are no funding or session-settlement transactions on client accounts.
  • Earn opt-in only: on Transfer to Futures add A2 → A1 (un-sweep) before the internal alloc; on Transfer from Futures add A1 → A2 (re-sweep) after the internal alloc.

Execution flow Certs client-side · 100% hedged issuer-side

Example. Client buys 10 KO certificates (BTC-LONG-KO63k, ratio 0.1 → delta ≈ 1 BTC), later sells at profit. Client orders trade only certs against the Issuer; the Issuer hedges each fill with a perp position against Nexo; Nexo may hedge externally at its own discretion.

Worked example — the certificate

  • Series: BTC-LONG-KO63k · open-end knock-out (turbo long) · ratio 0.1 · strike = barrier = 63,000 USDC.
  • Price: (BTC spot − 63,000) × 0.1 → 700 USDC per cert at spot 70,000 (≈10× effective leverage at issue).
  • Delta: 10 certs ≈ 1 BTC exposure → the Issuer hedges with a 1 BTC perp against Nexo.
  • Financing: baked into the 8h strike/barrier adjustment — no funding cash flows, no session settlement for the client.
  • Knock-out: if spot touches 63,000 the cert terminates at residual value (0 in the examples below).

Atomic transactions (money flow recipes)

Each recipe lists internal sub-ledger moves, T+0 book entries, and SQS. Earn variants only where noted. There are no funding recipes: positive / negative funding does not exist for certs — it is baked into the certificate terms.

Transfer to Futures Earn opt-in / opt-out

Example: Client allocates 10,000 USDC to the trading wallet.

Earn opt-in (A1 starts at 0):

  1. T+0: Debit 10,000 USDC from A2 → Credit A1.
  2. Internal: move 10,000 USDC from (1.1) Free → (1.2) Futures. Split: 0 Free / 10,000 Futures
  3. SQS: Client deposited 10,000 USDC to trading wallet.

Earn opt-out (funds already in A1):

  1. No Tangany TX.
  2. Internal: (1.1) Free → (1.2) Futures (10,000 USDC).
  3. SQS: same.

Transfer from Futures Earn opt-in / opt-out

Example: Client withdraws 10,000 USDC from the trading wallet.

  1. Internal: move 10,000 USDC from (1.2) Futures → (1.1) Free. Split: 10,000 Free / 0 Futures
  2. Opt-in: T+0 Debit A1 → Credit A2 (10,000 USDC). Opt-out: no Tangany TX.
  3. SQS: 10,000 USDC transferred out of trading wallet.

Buy KO certificates (outbound purchase, DvP)

Example: Client buys 10 certs at 700 USDC = 7,000 USDC.

  1. Pledge — before buying: German client pledges collateral to the Issuer; non-German client pledges to DLT (DLT MAY pledge onward to the Issuer). Book-entry only, never to Nexo.
  2. Internal: (1.2) Futures → (1.1) Free (7,000 USDC).
  3. T+0 cash leg — German: Debit 7,000 USDC from A1 → Credit A4 (direct). Non-German: Debit 7,000 USDC from A1 → Credit A3, then immediately Debit 7,000 USDC from A3 → Credit A4 (always via A3 first, even if only for a second). (1.1) returns to 0
  4. T+0 book: Debit 10 certs from A4 → Credit A1; internal certs (1.1) → (1.2).
  5. SQS: Client bought 10 BTC-LONG-KO63k at 700. DLT records the security-token purchase at T+0; the Issuer records the token ledger movement.
  6. Issuer-side: Issuer opens the hedge perp against Nexo out of A4 (never touches client accounts).

Sell KO certificates / Knock-out redemption (inbound, DvP)

Example: Client sells 10 certs at 1,500 USDC = 15,000 USDC. (A knock-out is the same recipe with proceeds = residual value, 0 in our examples.)

  1. Internal: certs (1.2) Futures → (1.1) Free.
  2. T+0 book: Debit 10 certs from A1 → Credit A4 (returned and cancelled).
  3. T+0 cash leg — German: Debit 15,000 USDC from A4 → Credit A1 (direct). Non-German: Debit 15,000 USDC from A4 → Credit A3, then immediately Debit 15,000 USDC from A3 → Credit A1 (always via A3 first). Internal (1.1) → (1.2).
  4. SQS: Client sold 10 BTC-LONG-KO63k at 1,500. DLT and Issuer record the ledger movements.
  5. Issuer-side: Issuer closes the hedge perp against Nexo.

Trading fee (outbound)

Example: 42 USDC trading fee on purchase (0.06% of 70,000 notional). Fees exist on every cert purchase and sale; there is no funding fee, session settlement or liquidation fee.

  1. Internal: (1.2) Futures → (1.1) Free (42 USDC).
  2. T+0 book: Debit A1 → Credit A3 (42 USDC). (1.1) returns to 0
  3. SQS: Client charged 42 USDC trading fee.

Collateral swap (multi-asset)

Triggered automatically whenever a USDC obligation (cert purchase price / trading fee) cannot be fully covered by the available USDC in (1.2) Futures. Sells just enough of an available non-USDC collateral asset (at current mark) to cover the shortage, then the original outbound transaction stands covered.

Example: Sell 0.1006 BTC @ 70,000 for 7,042 USDC to cover a cert purchase + fee.

  1. Internal: 0.1006 BTC (1.2) → (1.1) Free.
  2. T+0: Debit 0.1006 BTC A1 → Credit A3.
  3. T+0: Debit 7,042 USDC A3 → Credit A1.
  4. Internal: 7,042 USDC (1.1) → (1.2) Futures.
  5. SQS: BTC → USDC swap.
