Prediction Markets — securitised, as tokenised certificates

DLT Finance
Payoff profile · YES certificate Settlement value vs. outcome
Issued as a certificate on the same rails as everything else on this shelf — the graded payoff is what keeps it a security rather than a binary option.
Key Terms
FormSecuritised certificate (token)
ClassificationTransferable security, MiFID
PayoffGraded €0.00 → €1.00 across band
Price€0.00 – €1.00 = implied odds
ReferenceNamed official source / index
Revenue modelFee on notional traded
Why it belongs on this shelf
Pull
Highest-engagement product in the market right now Non-correlated to price direction — it trades on news flow, so it monetises flat and falling markets.
Reality
The wrapper is what makes it distributable Securitised, it moves through your existing MiFID broker rails — same custody, same KID, same API as the rest of the shelf.
WATCH-OUT · Securitising is necessary but not sufficient. ESMA's retail prohibition bites on the binary payoff, not the wrapper — an all-or-nothing certificate is still a binary option in substance and several NCAs read it that way. The graded band above is what takes it out of scope: economically a tight call spread, disclosed as a certificate. Classification to be confirmed with counsel per jurisdiction before a launch date is committed.
