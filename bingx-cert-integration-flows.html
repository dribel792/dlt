BingX × DLT × Anduin — Integration Proposal

Securitized Perps on BingX: Technical Integration Flow & Flow of Funds

October 2026 · Working draft v0.3 — companion to the BingX Listing Primer; adapts the KO Certificate Trading Flow (Nexo model, July 2026) to the BingX relationship

This document answers the two questions from the kick-off: what does the integration look like on a technical level — who does what in this relationship — and what does the flow of funds look like. Part A presents the two options for pricing-data provision and execution (Flow 1: perp books in, Anduin builds the cert book · Flow 2: cert books in, full STP). Part B presents the flow of funds with BingX EU (BingX's MiCAR entity) as custodian. Names and payload shapes are indicative; the goal is to agree the shape.

A1 · Actors — who does what

PartyRole & responsibilityWhat they run technically
BingX (frontend) Operates the client-facing UI / API under a tied-agent appointment from DLT. Owns the user relationship and order entry; RTOs / STPs orders to DLT. No execution, no pricing, no risk. UI/API (perp-style surface per the Listing Primer), order transmission to DLT, rendering of the quote / levels / docs feeds.
BingX Tied Agent Entity The BingX entity appointed by DLT as its tied agent — the legal face of the distribution. Holds an account at BingX EU and receives its share of the trading fee from DLT. No real-time systems in the flow.
DLT (broker) Executing broker and tied-agent principal — appoints the BingX Tied Agent Entity as its tied agent, which makes BingX's retail order transmission in a security lawful. Consumes Anduin's quote stream and serves it to the BingX UI; receives the RTO'd orders and passes them to Anduin as STP / prop (back-to-back, riskless principal), adding its spread or fee at this hop. Records every cert trade at T+0; regulatory reporting counterparty; collects the trading fee and credits the agreed share to the BingX Tied Agent Entity. Since no other venue lists these tokens, back-to-back execution against the issuer satisfies best execution. Market-data redistribution (WS/FIX in from Anduin, stream out to BingX), order routing, T+0 securities trade book, reporting rail, fee accounting.
Anduin (issuer) Issuer of record. Owns the golden record per series, generates and publishes Final Terms + PRIIPs KID, mints the tokens, is the counterparty of every client cert trade, runs the 8h funding sync (strike/barrier), monitors and declares knock-outs, computes residual values. Flow 1: consumes perp books from the MM and constructs the cert books itself; hedges every fill with a perp trade on the MM. Flow 2: consumes cert books from the MM and STPs every order to the MM. Flat market risk in both. Registry + doc-gen, token contracts, golden record DB, pricing/booking engine (Flow 1) or STP router (Flow 2), lifecycle daemon (funding + KO), WS/FIX market-data service to DLT, public levels/docs endpoints, append-only archive.
MM (BingX MM) Liquidity provider to Anduin. Flow 1: streams perp books (WS/FIX) and takes Anduin's perp hedge trades. Flow 2: streams cert books incl. the underlying markets and takes the STP'd cert orders directly. Manages the resulting market risk at its own discretion. The MM is the BingX MM, so the flow economics stay inside BingX — the internalization story from the Listing Primer. Pricing engine (perps or certs), WS/FIX market-data + execution API towards Anduin, own hedging infrastructure, margin account management (see Part B).
BingX EU (MiCAR entity · custodian) BingX's EU MiCAR entity and the custodian of the assets. User, DLT, Anduin and the BingX Tied Agent Entity all hold accounts at BingX EU. All client-side money and token movements are book entries between BingX EU accounts; assets never leave the custody perimeter. Account/booking API, "leverage trading" assignment flags, statements, rebalancing files.

A2 · Pricing & execution — two options

Both options use the same order chain (BingX → DLT → Anduin) and the same fee logic (DLT adds and collects). They differ in what the MM streams and therefore in who prices the certificate and who carries which risk.

Flow 1 — MM streams perp books Anduin pricesFlow 2 — MM streams cert books MM prices
Market data in Perp books of the reference markets, WS/FIX from the MM to Anduin. Cert books (incl. underlying markets), WS/FIX from the MM to Anduin.
Cert book construction Anduin derives the cert book from the perp book via the Final Terms formula on current levels. MM constructs the cert book, keyed to Anduin's published levels; Anduin validates and passes through.
Execution leg to the MM Perp hedge trade per client fill; MM confirms the perp execution price; Anduin derives and confirms the cert execution price to DLT. Cert order STP'd to the MM; MM confirms the cert execution price directly.
Anduin's residual risk Flat post-hedge; carries the quote-to-hedge latency/slippage gap. Fully riskless — instrument-level back-to-back per fill.
MM requirements Any perp MM works — no securities knowledge needed. MM must price and deal the security itself (bilateral professional-counterparty dealing under the MM agreement).
Who can be the MM Always the BingX MM.

Hosting

All of this activity can be hosted in the same datacenter for latency optimization — but every hop must run through the respective party's own servers (BingX's order gateway, DLT's broker stack, Anduin's issuer stack, the MM's engine). Co-location compresses latency; it does not collapse the chain into one operator — each entity demonstrably operates its own system.

A3 · Series creation — BingX requests, Anduin issues

Before anything can be quoted or traded, the certificate series has to exist. BingX initially requests the creation of a cert series (new underlying, direction or leverage band — e.g. "BTC long, 30×") via the Anduin Issuance API. Anduin runs the full issuance on its side and hands back everything BingX needs to render and trade the product. The same machinery creates the replacement series automatically after every knock-out, so the UI can immediately offer "trade the new 30×".

Sequence

  1. Series request — BingX → Anduin API. Parameter set: underlying + identifiers, direction (long/short), target leverage / KO level (initial strike & barrier), token count (max size), maintenance buffer, ratio, reference price source. MVP note may be a manual structurer flow initially; the API is the target state — the parameter set and validation rules are identical either way.
  2. Validation & golden record. Anduin validates the request against the base prospectus constraints (eligible underlyings, leverage band, reference venues) and creates the golden record — the single source of truth for every downstream artifact.
  3. Document creation. Anduin generates the Final Terms (deterministically from the golden record) and the PRIIPs KID (SRI, performance scenarios, cost tables) and publishes both — publication legally opens the offer and must precede any purchasable cert.
  4. Token mint. Anduin deploys the token and mints the series size to its inventory; series goes live.
  5. Response — Anduin → BingX. series_id, token_address, Final Terms URL, KID URL. BingX renders the doc links in the UI — the KID one click away from the order ticket (hard regulatory requirement at point of sale).
  6. Quoting commences. The MM starts streaming books for the new series (perp books in Flow 1, cert books in Flow 2) and the pricing chain of A2 takes over.

B · Flow of funds — BingX EU as custodian

BingX works with BingX EU as their MiCAR entity, which is the custodian of the assets. User, DLT, Anduin and the BingX Tied Agent Entity each hold an account at BingX EU. Client-side cash and token movements are book entries between these accounts. The margin relationship towards the MM (BingX MM) runs alongside it and is trued up by a periodic rebalancing (T+1 as the working example).

B2 · Phase 1 — Setup & pre-trade (before any order)

Sequence

  1. Deposit. The user funds their account at BingX EU (User Account).
  2. Assignment to "leverage trading". The user assigns part of their BingX EU balance to leverage trading — a book-only earmark inside custody, like funding the futures wallet today.
  3. Assignment info → DLT. The assignment (amount, client reference, timestamps) is shared with DLT, so DLT can perform POSOF checks (proof of source of funds) where needed before accepting order flow against that balance.
  4. Mirroring on BingX MM. All funds the user credits to leverage trading are mirrored by BingX on BingX MM as available margin / collateral for Anduin — i.e. BingX makes equivalent margin capacity available on the MM so Anduin's hedge/STP leg can execute instantly when the user trades, without waiting for settlement. The user's actual assets stay in BingX EU custody throughout — the mirror is capacity on the MM side, not a movement of client assets.

B3 · Phase 2 — Purchase (worked example: 10 certs à 700 USDC, fee 0.06%)

Sequence

  1. User buys 10 certs via the BingX UI; the order runs the chain of Part A (BingX RTO → DLT STP/prop → Anduin → MM).
  2. Cash leg: at the time of purchase the user's funds are credited to DLT including the fee (7,000 + 4.20 USDC), then credited onward to Anduin without the fee (7,000 USDC). T+0 book entries at BingX EU.
  3. Fee split: DLT retains its share and credits the agreed part of the fee to the BingX Tied Agent Entity's BingX EU account.
  4. Security leg (DvP): 10 certs move Anduin Account → User Account.
  5. Pledge: Anduin pledges the received funds to BingX — this is the collateral backing the margin capacity BingX mirrored on BingX MM in Phase 1 (utilized portion).
  6. MM leg: Anduin's hedge (Flow 1) or STP'd cert order (Flow 2) executes on BingX MM against that margin.

B4 · Phase 3 — T+1 rebalancing of realized PnL

Sequence (T+1 as the working example — cadence is a parameter)

  1. Over the day, client sells / knock-outs realize PnL on Anduin's positions at BingX MM.
  2. Positive realized PnL on BingX MM is moved back to BingX EU (BingX MM → Anduin Account @ BingX EU) — from there it funds client sale proceeds / KO residual payouts.
  3. Negative realized PnL is topped up in the other direction (Anduin Account @ BingX EU → BingX MM), keeping the margin account whole.
  4. The mirrored margin capacity is re-trued to the current sum of user leverage-trading assignments; Anduin's pledge is re-trued to utilized positions.
